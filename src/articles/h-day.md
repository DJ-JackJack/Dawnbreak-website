On the morning of Sunday, June 11, 2028, [[Dr. Erroghastor]] released
**BEV-131** over the [[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]]. It
did not stay there. The virus reached the rest of the country and eventually the
rest of the world, and twelve years later Dawnbreak is still its epicentre. The
date is a proper noun here and a news item everywhere else.

## What Happened

BEV-131 is the Blood-born Evolutionary Virus. Erroghastor built it and released
it into the airspace above the district, and the people directly underneath were
the first affected anywhere.

He has never said why. Twelve years of custody and questioning have produced no
motive and no explanation of any part of it, including the name.

The virus spread uncontrolled from the moment of release. It causes mutation,
disfigurement and new abilities, and nobody has learned to predict which of those
a given person will get. What is established is that BEV-131 does not select. It
has no preference and picks no one. Outcomes track exposure time, and the longer
someone stood in it the more likely it changed them.

## The Response

Every emergency service in Dawnbreak went into the district, along with the
volunteer companies from the districts around it. They went in to bring people
out, which meant working inside an exposure zone nobody at the time had the
instruments to measure.

They were therefore the last to leave, and they were affected out of all
proportion to their numbers for that reason alone. Exposure time is the whole of
the explanation.

Several people who later became public figures were in the district that morning,
and their accounts have been retold heavily since.
[[The Herculean|Dave Giannopoulos]] was a firefighter three years into the job
and went in with his company. [[Coldstreak|Amee Song]] was fourteen, already
there, mid-routine in front of an audience. [[Lackluster|Joseph Flint]] held a
building lobby against people who had begun attacking those around them.
[[Spotlight|Richard Pierre]] had been working as a Dawnbreak paramedic for less
than a month.

## Aftermath

The district itself came through. It was not destroyed, reconstruction was fast
and well funded, and it was structurally whole again inside a decade. What it
lost was its business, which moved to [[New Haven]] during the rebuild and stayed
there.

In the couple of years after H-Day, superpowered attacks became ordinary. Some of
it was criminal, people using abilities they had just been handed. Some of it was
not crime in any useful sense, but people who had lost control of what the virus
had done to them hurting whoever was nearby. Ordinary police could not handle either
kind, and that gap is the reason heroes stepped up. It was years before any of
them formed a team.

BEV-131 is still active in 2040. Outbreaks are rarer than they were and they
still happen, and mutation remains one of the larger causes of metahuman
violence. There are no confirmed cases of the virus evolving.

Erroghastor was captured and imprisoned, and he is still alive in 2040.

## How It's Remembered

June 11 is marked and not celebrated. There are ceremonies, there are moments of
silence, there is coverage, and most of the city goes to work. For people who
were in the district, and for the families of the dead, it is a day to get
through rather than a day to observe. Round-numbered anniversaries draw more
attention. 2040 is the twelfth and a quieter one than 2038 was.

The anniversary coverage is produced by [[Arkon Media]], whose hero team's entire
public origin is that morning. The commemoration and the brand are the same
content, and nobody has to intend anything for that to be uncomfortable.

And for the people BEV-131 changed, June 11 is also the anniversary of the day
they were changed. That is not what the ceremonies are about, and nothing marks
it.

## The Public Record

The date, the location, the agent and the man responsible are all established and
uncontested.

The toll is not. No figure has ever been settled on. Deaths on the day, deaths in
the secondary chaos, deaths afterward, and a changed population that is still not
fully counted twelve years on all resist a single number, and different
institutions publish different ones.

Erroghastor's motive is the largest gap in the record and has been since the day
itself. He is available to be asked, and he has been asked.
