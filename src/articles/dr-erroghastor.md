Seymour Harold Erroghastor built BEV-131 and released it over the
[[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]] on the morning of
[[H-Day]]. The virus reached the rest of the country and then the rest of the
world. He was the first person to do anything of the kind, he has been in
custody since, and in twelve years he has never said why.

## Emergence

He held doctorates in bio-engineering, biochemistry, genetics, virology and
psychology. Four of them are the virus. The fifth built nothing.

Every account of him written since begins on the same morning. He is dated from
June 11, 2028, and the life before it survives as a list of qualifications.

The word supervillain entered ordinary use with him. It arrived because
something had happened that needed a word, and the man it was coined for could
do nothing at all.

## Method

BEV-131 is the Blood-born Evolutionary Virus, engineered rather than found. He
released it into the airspace above the district, and the people directly
beneath it were the first affected anywhere.

It spread uncontrolled from that moment. It produces mutation, disfigurement and
new abilities, and which of the three a person receives has stayed beyond
prediction. What is established is that the virus selects for one thing only.
Outcomes track exposure time: the longer somebody stood in it, the likelier it
changed them.

That is the whole of the method. One release, and everything after it followed
on its own.

## Capabilities

He has no abilities and never had any. He survived his own release by standing
somewhere else.

What he had was five doctorates and the willingness to use them once. He is
recorded as having acted alone, and twelve years of investigation have left that
finding standing.

## Encounters

There is one. He acted on June 11, 2028, was taken into custody afterward, and
has been held ever since, so the register runs to a single entry.

The record holds that he was taken alive and says very little about how.

## Custody

He is in federal custody and he is alive.

Where he is held stays withheld, along with the arrangements and the terms. That
withholding has been the most consistent official position anyone has taken
about him: the public is told that he is secured, that he will remain so, and
that the rest is a matter of security.

## The Public Record

He is named. Fully, publicly, and with his doctorates attached, which makes him
unusual among people who have done what he did, since most are recorded by what
they are called rather than by who they are. The alias is simply his surname
with the title he earned.

The gap is the motive. Twelve years of custody and questioning have produced no
explanation of the release, the choice of district, the timing, or the name he
gave the thing he built. He has been asked by investigators, prosecutors,
physicians and journalists. He has answered none of them, and the silence
extends to everyone who might have spoken for him.

That silence is the most examined thing about him, and it has outlasted every
theory advanced to explain it.
