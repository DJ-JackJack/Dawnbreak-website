Arkon Media is older than the city it reports on. It began in 1911 as a
newspaper in New York, it followed the people who left New York to build
[[Dawnbreak City]], and in 2040 it owns the city's primary news source and
employs its most famous residents.

## Founding

Vance Rittenbough and Darrel Cocchetti started the paper in New York City in
1911. It was a small operation and it remained one for over a decade.

What changed it was a migration. In the early 1920s people fleeing persecution
in New York went south to found a city on the Virginia coast, and Rittenbough
and Cocchetti went with them and kept publishing. The paper covered the
departure, the arrival and the years of hardship that came after, at a length
and with a consistency nobody else bothered with.

Dawnbreak has therefore never existed without Arkon reporting on it. The company
predates the city by roughly a decade, arrived with the founders, and has been
the record of the place since before there was a place to record. It is the
first fact in any account Arkon gives of itself, and it is true.

## Structure

Amelia Sanabria is chief executive. The company is worth many millions and is
run out of Olympus Tower in [[New Haven]], a building it owns and is not shy
about owning.

The top two floors are [[Pantheon]]'s headquarters and dormitory. The floors
beneath them hold the offices of Arkon's senior staff, and the basement holds
the company's research and development facility. The campus surrounding the
tower was built to take public events at scale.

## Operations

Arkon runs two businesses and they have grown into each other.

The first is news. Arkon owns [[The Dawnbreak Trumpet]], which is what most of
Dawnbreak means when it says it saw something in the news. The Trumpet is still
described as a newspaper and has not been printed on anything for years.
Newsprint is a dead medium by 2040, and what the Trumpet actually consists of is
a very large social presence, a set of news applications and a slate of shows.
The name is inherited rather than descriptive.

The second is heroes. Arkon funds Pantheon, houses it, and manages what the
public sees of it. The arrangement is the standard one: direct government
funding for heroes is rare, and a company that sponsors a team collects tax
relief, grants, access to government contracts and a considerable quantity of
public goodwill. [[Cross Idea Network]] runs a smaller operation on the same
model.

[[Oracle Prime|Oracle]] came out of the first business and went into the second.
Arkon built it as a broadcast system, an automated operator for film shoots and
news coverage that decided where to point, when to cut and how to frame a face
so it read the way the studio wanted. The All Seeing Eye drones carried cameras
before they carried anything else.

## In the City

Arkon presents Oracle as female, maternal and attentive: the All Seeing Eyes
overhead, watching the whole city, with Pantheon a moment behind them. The
stated aim is to make her familiar to Dawnbreak. It has worked. Twelve years of
metahuman violence and the standing possibility of another outbreak have left a
city that badly wants to be looked after, and Arkon has answered that more
directly than any other institution here.

The company also produces the [[H-Day]] anniversary coverage. It owns the
primary news source, it employs the most visible survivors of that morning, and
it makes the programmes through which the city remembers the day. None of those
three facts is disputed and they are not often stated together.

Arkon is one of six corporations that funded [[Tartarus Sands Penitentiary]],
alongside the Iker-Graves Corporation and the federal government.

## The Public Record

The company's filings are public. So, necessarily, is everything it broadcasts.

The terms are not. Arrangements between Arkon and individual members of Pantheon
are private commercial agreements and none has been disclosed. Nothing about the
research and development facility under the tower has been published beyond the
fact that it is there.
