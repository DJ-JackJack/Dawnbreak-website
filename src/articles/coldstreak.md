Amee Song was fourteen years old and performing on the ice in the
[[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]] when [[Dr. Erroghastor]]
released BEV-131 over it. She is second generation despite that, because she did
not begin hero work until 2032. She is the reason the generational line is drawn
where it is.

## June 11, 2028

She was mid-routine, in front of an audience, when it happened.

[[The Herculean|Dave Giannopoulos]] chose to run toward ground zero. Amee Song
was simply already standing on it, doing the thing she had been doing since
childhood, at an ordinary Sunday exhibition. She was among the first people
anywhere exposed to BEV-131, and she was fourteen.

## The Decision

Four years passed between the day the virus changed her and the day she started.
She turned eighteen in 2032 and began then. As of 2040 she has been active for
eight years and powered for twelve.

What those four years held is not published and she has not discussed it.
Whatever they were, they were not spent in costume.

This is why generation in Dawnbreak is counted from when a person started rather
than from when they were changed. Coldstreak was powered on H-Day itself, in the
first minutes of it, and she is second generation. The rule is not a technicality
that happens to catch her. She is the case the rule was written around.

## Capabilities

Her speed does not work the way other speedsters' does. She draws ambient
kinetic energy out of her surroundings and burns it to move, which means the
motion she uses has to come from somewhere, and it comes from the air around
her.

The cold is exhaust. Pulling the movement out of a space leaves that space
dangerously cold, and she has learned to aim the by-product: ice in her wake, or
ice as a weapon.

The ice is not confined to the ground. She directs it into shapes as she needs
them, and ramps, paths and blocks all get built mid-run. Difficult terrain is
less of a problem for her than for almost anyone else on the team, because she
does not wait to find a route.

Two things about the cold matter more than any of that.

The first is that it only exists while her speed is running. Powered down she is
safe to stand next to, and this is where her old life stops being backstory and
becomes equipment: she skates on the ice she has just made, using a skill she has
had since she was a child to keep moving quickly with her powers switched off.
That is how she gets to people, gets them out, and works in a crowd without
hurting anyone in it.

The second is persistence. While her speed is active, ice near her holds
indefinitely, because the power keeps drawing off the warmth that would melt it.
The moment she powers down or moves away, it melts like any other ice. She
cannot brace a collapsing structure and pull somebody out from under it at the
same time, because the state that holds the ice up is the state that makes her
dangerous to touch. Every serious rescue she takes is a countdown, and it starts
when she stops running.

## In the City

She runs a free skating programme on Saturday mornings at the rink on
[[Grid Row]]'s inland edge. It is open to anyone under sixteen, it has been
running for years, and it is not an [[Arkon Media]] initiative.

It is also the only part of her life outside Pantheon that she has made public,
and she has kept it small enough that it has stayed hers.

## The Public Record

Her name and her H-Day account are both published and both corroborated. The
exhibition she was performing at is documented, and there were several hundred
people watching.

The four years between are the gap. No part of that period appears in any
published profile of her, Arkon's included, and she has declined to fill it in
on the occasions she has been asked directly.
