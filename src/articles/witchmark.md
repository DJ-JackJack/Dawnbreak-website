Marielle Moss is the oldest member of [[Pantheon]] and the one who runs its
fights. On [[H-Day]] she was forty-five, working as a software developer in
[[Arkon Media]]'s games division, in a studio that stood in the middle of the
[[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]]. She nearly died in it.

## June 11, 2028

The studio was close enough to the release that the people in it had no useful
warning and no good options.

The account Arkon publishes has her abilities arriving as the building came down
around her, and has her using them to get colleagues out of it. That account
appears in every published profile of her, including her own tellings of it.

What is not in dispute is that she was forty-five years old, in an office, on a
Sunday, and that she came out of a building most of the way through collapsing.

## The Decision

She is the only member of Pantheon who was already working for Arkon Media
before she was working for Arkon Media. The others were recruited. She was on the
payroll of the games division, with twelve years of credits behind her, and her
employer and her sponsor have been the same company from the first day to this
one.

She was also forty-five, which is not the age at which people generally take up
a second career involving structural collapse. Pantheon was founded ten years
later, in 2038, and she has been with it since.

## Capabilities

She is an adept magician, and she is the reason Pantheon can meet a magical
threat at all. Every other member of the roster is, in the end, a person who
hits things extremely hard or moves extremely fast. Against a category of
problem that does not answer to either, she is the entire response.

That alone would earn her the place. What earns her the job she actually holds
is a sharp intellect and a strong tactical read, which together make her the
team's field commander.

Leadership of Pantheon belongs to [[Spotlight]] and has since the team was
founded. The division is clean and both of them describe it the same way. She
runs the fight. He runs the team.

## In the City

She is credited as one of the designers of [[Oracle Prime|Oracle]], which is a
line in her public record that nobody outside the company has ever been able to
put much detail behind.

She reads, in coverage, as the adult in a team of extremely powerful young
people, and the city noticed that before Arkon found a way to sell it. The
nickname arrived from outside and the company has used it since.

She is not a public figure in the way [[Spotlight]] or [[The Herculean]] are.
She appears where the work puts her and very little else, and the profile she
keeps is markedly lower than her seniority on the team would predict.

## The Public Record

Half of her life is documented exhaustively. Twelve years at an Arkon games
studio produce credits, filings, colleagues and a paper trail, and all of it is
available and consistent.

The half before that is thin. What is published about Marielle Moss prior to her
employment at Arkon amounts to very little, and the profiles that have tried to
fill it in have had nothing to work from. She has not offered anything, and no
one has pressed her on it in print.
