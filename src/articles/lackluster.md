Joseph Flint was sitting in a lobby in a good shirt, waiting to be called in for
a job interview, when [[H-Day]] happened around him. He held that lobby for the
rest of the morning against people who had begun attacking anyone near them, and
he held it with no abilities of any kind. He is [[Pantheon]]'s infiltration
specialist now. He had nothing to infiltrate anything with until late that
summer.

## June 11, 2028

He was thirty, a former Green Beret and army sniper with three children at home,
and he was in the [[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]] to
interview for a job.

When it broke he took command of the lobby and kept it. Twenty-four civilians
came out of that building alive because a man with no powers decided the room
was his responsibility and then made that hold for as long as it needed to. What
he had was his training, whatever was in the room, and thirty years of knowing
what to do when everything goes wrong at once.

His own abilities did not surface until roughly two months later.

## The Decision

The order of events is most of what people find interesting about him. He did
the thing he is known for first, and the powers arrived afterward. The record can
point at one morning and one lobby and say plainly that none of it required a
metahuman.

What he does now requires one. He joined Pantheon at its founding in 2038 and
has been the team's stealth and infiltration specialist since.

## Capabilities

### Intangibility

He phases, and he can carry nonorganic material through with him. Organic matter
does not travel, which rules out wood and a good deal of cloth, and it rules out
other people altogether.

That exclusion inverts what intangibility is usually worth. Steel, concrete,
glass and rebar are all open to him. A wooden door is a wall.

It also means ordinary clothing would not survive the trip. His working suit is
a nylon and synthetic kevlar mesh, synthetic precisely so that it phases when he
does, and [[Arkon Media]] is the one that makes it. His kit is a bespoke
material rather than a costume choice.

### Shadowmask

Shadowmask is his own name for the second ability. If he makes contact with a
person's shadow, he can take their exact form as it stood at the moment he
touched it, clothing and all.

It is a snapshot rather than an identity. He gains none of their abilities and
none of what they know, and he knows only what Joseph Flint already knew. The
disguise survives being looked at. It collapses the moment somebody asks him a
question only the real person could answer.

## In the City

The public named him. Somebody started calling him the Pantheon's Ninja, it
stuck, and Arkon took the name it had been handed and built a suit to match,
styled after Hollywood shinobi. The company's infiltration specialist works in
the most recognisable silhouette available, which is a decision with an obvious
logic behind it and not an operational one.

Arkon's interest in him runs in two directions at once. He is marketable, which
the Ninja business demonstrates. He is also an infiltration capability, and a
company that owns one has something none of its competitors can buy off a shelf.

## The Public Record

The lobby is the best-documented thing about him, and the account of it does not
rest on his own telling. Twenty-four people walked out of that building and the
number has never been disputed.

His service record is public in outline and closed in detail, as service records
are. His three children have been kept out of the coverage.
