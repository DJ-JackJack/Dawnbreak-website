Pantheon is Dawnbreak City's own hero team and the most visible in the country.
Six members, founded in 2038, funded by [[Arkon Media]], and housed on the top
two floors of Olympus Tower in [[New Haven]]. Most of what the city thinks it
knows about heroes, it knows from watching these six.

## Founding

Teams were not the first shape heroism took. For years after [[H-Day]] the
people who chose to help did it alone, with no coordination and no collective
name for what they were doing. [[Vanguard]], founded in 2033 in the Pacific
Northwest, was the first official hero team anywhere. Pantheon came five years
after that, which means Dawnbreak spent a decade as the city where it happened
and had no team of its own.

Membership is decided on power. Pantheon draws from both the first and second
generations, and the generational line is not a criterion.

## Structure

[[Spotlight]] leads. He is the most powerful member and the team's public face,
and nothing in the record suggests the role rotates.

Pantheon is a corporate asset as well as a hero team. Arkon funds it, houses it
and manages what the public sees of it. That arrangement is ordinary in 2040
rather than particular to Pantheon. Direct government funding for heroes is rare,
and companies have filled the gap: a firm that sponsors a team receives tax
relief, grants, access to government contracts, and a considerable amount of
public goodwill. The state still pays for heroism. It pays through the tax code
rather than the budget, and it lets the companies decide where the money points.

The dormitory holds twelve rooms. Each member has one and none of them are
required to use it. Six sit empty. Twelve mirrors the twelve primary Olympians.

## Operations

Olympus Tower is Arkon Media's building and the team occupies the top of it. The
floors below the dormitory hold the offices of Arkon's senior staff, and the
basement holds the company's research and development facility. The Arkon Media
campus surrounds the tower and is large enough to take a crowd, a press pack and
a queue of applicants at the same time.

[[Oracle Prime|Oracle]] is how the team is reached and how it is sent. The All
Seeing Eye drones operate in the hundreds and are overhead continuously, which
makes them the part of Pantheon most of the city actually sees. It also means a
Pantheon response can begin before anyone has called anyone.

## In the City

Pantheon is popular and the reason is not complicated. Twelve years of metahuman
violence and the standing possibility of another outbreak have left Dawnbreak
wanting to feel looked after, and Pantheon is what being looked after currently
looks like.

The team is also the clearest example of how unevenly the city treats the
changed. [[The Herculean]] is six foot nine, visibly mutated, and among the
best-liked people in Dawnbreak. In the [[The Old Financial District|Old
Financial District]], people whose mutations are far less obvious have trouble
renting a flat. Both of those are true at once and neither is a secret.

Threats at the level Pantheon exists to handle are uncommon without being rare,
and they outnumber the heroes who can meet them. That arithmetic is
the reason a team of six in one city is a national story.

## The Public Record

All six members are publicly credited to BEV-131, directly or indirectly, with
Oracle the acknowledged exception on the grounds of being an artificial
intelligence. Arkon presents that uniformity as the team's meaning: the city's
tragedy produced the city's protectors.

Rosters, appearances and after-action coverage are published by Arkon, which also
owns [[The Dawnbreak Trumpet]], the city's primary news source.

The six empty rooms are a matter of public record and have been since the
dormitory was first shown on camera. Arkon has never said what it intends to do
with them.
