Dave Giannopoulos was a Dawnbreak firefighter, born and raised in
[[Grid Row]], and on [[H-Day]] he went into the [[The Old Financial District|Old
Financial District]] with his company. He was three years into the job and had
no powers when he went in. What he has now, he got doing that work.

## June 11, 2028

He responded to ground zero. There is no more to the origin than that, and the
people who know him tend to say so with some impatience, because the question
usually arrives expecting something else.

He ran toward the worst thing that had ever happened to his city carrying
nothing that would have helped if it had gone differently. No invulnerability,
no resistance, no reason to think he was coming back out. It was the job, and he
was young enough in it to still be proving he could do it.

BEV-131 took him in the course of that work. His abilities are an occupational
exposure in the most literal sense the phrase allows.

## The Decision

He is first generation, and for him that meant almost no gap at all. He was
already a man who turned up for strangers on the worst day of theirs, and the
powers arrived after that was settled rather than before. What changed in 2028
was his capability, not his intentions.

Pantheon came ten years later. He joined at its founding in 2038 and has been
with [[Arkon Media]] since, on terms that are a working arrangement rather than
a cause. He supports his mother and his disabled sister, and he still lives in
Grid Row with them rather than in the room he keeps in Olympus Tower.

## Capabilities

Strength and durability, and very little else. He is the team's heaviest hitter
in the plainest sense: Pantheon uses him to hit things, not to stand in front of
them. A man that strong connects like a vehicle, and the tactics follow from
that.

His invulnerability is the highest on the team. His raw physical strength is at
or above [[Spotlight]]'s, which for a team led by the most powerful hero in the
country is a distinction worth stating carefully.

What he does not have is everything else. He cannot fly, he has no speed worth
the name, and he projects no energy of any kind. He arrives on foot or he
arrives in a vehicle, and there is no way to make either look good on camera.

BEV-131 rewrote his appearance more thoroughly than anyone else's on the team.
He stands six foot nine, with rough stony patches across his skin and a thick
mane of red hair. There is no passing for unchanged and no version of his life
in which he goes unnoticed in a room.

## In the City

Grid Row claims him and does it without much ceremony. That the man who works as
the Herculean grew up on those streets and worked out of a firehouse there is a
matter of public record, and the district treats it as ordinary in a way
[[Arkon Media]] would plainly prefer it did not.

Across Dawnbreak he is among the most popular public figures of any kind. He is
also the most visibly changed. Those two facts sitting together is the single
most quoted thing about him, usually by people making a point about how the city
treats everybody else it can see was changed.

## The Public Record

His civilian name is published and has been for years, which makes him unusual
among heroes at his level. His H-Day account is corroborated by his company and
by the department, and no part of it has ever been seriously questioned.

His family is known about and is not covered. Arkon handles his appearances, and
what reaches the public about him is what an Arkon-managed career produces: a
great deal of footage, very little from the man himself.
