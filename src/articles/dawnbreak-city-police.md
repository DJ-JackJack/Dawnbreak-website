The Dawnbreak City Police is the city's only police force, headquartered two
streets from the courthouse in [[Aldergate]] and answerable to a council of nine
and the manager that council appoints. It polices a city where some of the
people it is responsible for can lift a bus.

## Founding

The department is as old as the city. Dawnbreak was laid out in the early 1920s
and the founders drew the courthouse and the police headquarters within a few
minutes' walk of each other at the top of Court Street, which is where both have
stayed. Nobody has revisited the arrangement.

Dawnbreak is an independent city in the Virginia sense and sits inside no county,
so the department has no sheriff to divide work with and no county force
overlapping it. What it has is the whole city and everything in it.

## Structure

The department is organised the way a city force of its size anywhere would be,
with one addition that postdates [[H-Day]].

The **Metahuman Containment Unit** is a specialist unit raised to answer
metahuman calls. It carries militarised equipment, high-powered firearms, and
weapons built for specific known power types. It is the unit dispatched when a
call is already understood to be beyond an ordinary response.

Underneath the MCU sits something broader and less discussed. Metahuman
countermeasure training and heavier protective equipment were issued force-wide,
not to a unit. Every officer in Dawnbreak carries the training and the gear. The
department decided early that a metahuman call does not announce itself before
the first car arrives, and it equipped accordingly.

## Operations

Cooperation with heroes is departmental policy and is not optional. At the level
of how the city actually runs, the two work together, and the arrangement is
stable.

At the level of individual officers, feeling about it is mixed and always has
been. Heroes can do things a beat cop cannot, which is a fact no one in the
building pretends otherwise about. The department knows exactly where its limits
are, and the countermeasures it has adopted barely move them. The officers
carrying the gear understand that better than anyone. It buys minutes and
occasionally a life, and it does not close the gap.

What grates is the comparison itself. The department is measured, continually and
publicly, against a cohort of people who can end an incident by arriving. Being
cast as the ones who could not handle it is a sore spot of long standing, and
twelve years has not worn it down.

## In the City

The department lost officers on H-Day. They went into the
[[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]] with every other emergency
service in Dawnbreak and stayed in it for the same reason everyone else did, and
they were exposed for as long as they stayed. The 2028 roll is on public display
in the headquarters building. St. Alder's in Aldergate displays its own, and
neither institution coordinated with the other about doing it.

Ordinary policing in Dawnbreak remains most of the job. The dramatic calls are a
small fraction of the work and generate nearly all of the coverage, which is its
own quiet grievance.

## The Public Record

The department publishes its H-Day account and its 2028 losses, as the hospitals
and the fire companies do.

The MCU's existence, remit and general equipment profile are public. The specific
counters it carries for specific power types are not published, and the
department has not said who maintains that information or how it is kept current.
