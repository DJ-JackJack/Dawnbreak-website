Richard Pierre was born in Haiti, raised in Miami, and had been living in
Dawnbreak City for under a month when [[Dr. Erroghastor]] released BEV-131 over
the [[The Old Financial District|Old Financial District]]. He had no family
here, no history with the place and no reason to stay beyond an address and a
start date. He leads [[Pantheon]].

## June 11, 2028

He was twenty-one, working as a paramedic and putting himself through medical
school, which is a schedule that leaves a person tired and in the wrong part of
the city at most hours of the day. On June 11 it put him where the ambulances
were going.

The city he went to work in that morning was one he barely knew. Three weeks is
not enough time to acquire the thing that makes people run toward their own
neighbourhood, and he went anyway, because the distinction between a stranger's
emergency and your own is not one paramedics are encouraged to make.

He had already chosen a life of turning up for people on the worst day of
theirs. What happened to him that morning changed what he could do about it and
changed nothing else.

## The Decision

There is less of a decision here than the phrase implies, which is true of most
of the first generation and unusually true of him. The vocation preceded the
powers by several years and survived them intact. He was doing this work before
BEV-131, he was doing it during, and the only thing that materially changed was
the scale at which he could do it.

He never finished medical school.

Pantheon was founded around him in 2038 and he has led it since. Ten years
separate the two facts, and what he did in them is the ordinary answer: he
worked, alone, the way everyone in the first generation worked, because there
were no teams to join.

## Capabilities

He is fuelled by solar energy, and everything else follows from that. Strength
and speed well beyond human limits, flight, and energy projected from his hands.
It is the most conventional power set on the team by a distance, the one a child
drawing a hero draws, and he is the most powerful member of Pantheon.

He is also, by the plainest possible mechanism, the most photogenic. He does not
train for it and Arkon does not manufacture it. A man who runs on sunlight
photographs well in sunlight.

The catch is written on a calendar. His power scales with the sun: stronger in
broad daylight, stronger again the nearer the clock runs to noon, and dependent
throughout on the sun actually being out. His best hours are the brightest ones
and anybody can work out when those are. A heavily overcast afternoon in
February is a different proposition from a clear noon in June, and he plans
around the difference in a way the coverage rarely shows.

## In the City

He is the face of Pantheon and, by extension, the face of heroism in Dawnbreak
for most people who do not think about it further. The team's public identity is
substantially his.

The Metahuman Incarceration Block at the bottom of
[[Tartarus Sands Penitentiary|Tartarus Sands]] was built with his help, and it
was built to hold someone operating at his ceiling for an extended period. He assisted in the
design of a cell specified against himself. This is published, and it is used by
Arkon as a statement about his character, which does not make it untrue.

## The Public Record

His name, his origin and his medical background are all published and none of it
has been contested. He is among the most extensively documented people in the
country.

What the record contains very little of is Richard Pierre. Twelve years of
coverage have produced an enormous quantity of footage and remarkably few of his
own words, and the arrangement appears to suit everybody involved.
