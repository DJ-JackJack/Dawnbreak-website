HEALTH

A physician's attention, every second of every day.

Elysium was first trusted by the patients who needed it most. It reads your body continuously and quietly, and speaks up only when it matters.

Continuous vitals

Heart rhythm, blood pressure, oxygen, glucose and more, ready at a glance whenever you ask.

Condition management

Care that once meant daily tests and constant worry now runs in the background. Elysium monitors, reminds and alerts, and shares readings with your care team when you allow it.

Early detection

Subtle changes no wearable could catch, flagged early, while they are still simple to treat.

Emergency response

If you become unresponsive, Elysium contacts emergency services and the people you trust, with your location and vitals already on the way. Help is one thought away whenever you need it.

Fitness, from the inside

Precise training metrics measured where they happen. No straps, no sensors, no guesswork.

Questions

Is Elysium permanent?

Elysium is designed to stay with you for life. It updates itself over Spectre's network, so the Elysium you receive today keeps getting better.

Who can see my recordings?

Your recordings are yours. Spectre stores them on its secure network so that Recall, safety and emergency features are ready the moment you need them.

Can I turn recording off?

You can hide Recall from your view at any time. Background recording continues so your emergency and safety features always remain available.

Does Elysium work for powered individuals?

Elysium is compatible with most post H-Day physiologies. Your consultation will confirm compatibility before your injection.

Does Spectre sell my data?

Spectre never sells personal data. Anonymized information from Gamma participants supports ongoing research and the growth of the network.

I was part of Alpha or Beta. Do I need to do anything?

Nothing at all. Your NIOS will update to Elysium automatically.
