# Canon notes

Setting facts that originated during site work, recorded here so they are not
lost in a chat log.

**`dawnbreak-canon.md` remains the source of truth.** Nothing in this file
outranks it. When something here is confirmed, it should move there; until it
does, treat it as settled-but-unfiled.

## The City of Changes

**Status: canon, from Krys, 2026-09-02.**

Dawnbreak City's long-standing nickname. It appears as the site's subtitle.

The phrase was **adopted by the refugees who fled persecution in New York and
founded the city**, early in its history. In their mouths it meant a place
where a life could be changed — arrival, escape, a second attempt.

**H-Day changed what it means.** After 11 June 2028 the same three words
describe something that happens *to* people rather than something they choose.
Nobody rebranded and nothing official was announced; the words simply stopped
meaning the first thing.

That the city kept saying it anyway is the interesting part.

### Why it matters beyond a tagline

It is the only piece of pre-H-Day identity in canon so far. Everything else in
`dawnbreak-canon.md` dates from 2027 onward, which has quietly made the city
feel as though it began in 2028. This gives it a founding, a reason to exist,
and a population that arrived with a motive — and it means H-Day took something
from the city that predated it.

It also gives every **Locations** and **Events** article a second axis. Not just
*what did H-Day do to this place*, but *what was this place for beforehand*.

### Open questions

Deliberately unanswered. Krys supplied the shape; these are the gaps, and none
of them should be filled by invention.

- **Who was persecuted, and for what?** Not specified, and probably load-bearing
  enough to be worth deciding deliberately rather than in passing.
- **When?** "Early on." The city has no founding date in canon yet. Several
  fields want one — a `built` value of "Pre-H-Day" is currently doing a lot of
  work that a real year would do better.
- **Is the phrase official or vernacular?** On a seal and a letterhead, or only
  ever in speech? The difference decides whether Arkon can use it.
- **Is there still a recognisable descendant community?** Two existing canon
  details point at one without confirming it: `dawnbreak-harriers.md` describes
  the Harbor as belonging to "the working population who founded the city", and
  `dawnbreak-canon.md` calls Grid Row "the oldest continuously working part of
  the city". Those may be the same people, or may not. **Not assumed here.**
- **Does anyone resent it?** A city that keeps a founding slogan after its
  meaning inverts is either being stubborn, sentimental, or grimly funny, and
  which one it is says a great deal about the place.

## How to use this file

Add an entry when a setting fact is decided while building the site rather than
in `dawnbreak-canon.md`. Include the date, who decided it, and the open
questions it opens — the last part matters most, because the questions are what
stop the next article from quietly answering them by accident.

Nothing in here is DM-only. This repository is public; campaign prep does not
belong in it. See `docs/ARTICLE-TEMPLATES.md`.
