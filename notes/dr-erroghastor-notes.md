# Notes on Dr. Erroghastor

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Krys supplied the name in full on 2026-09-12 — **Seymour Harold Erroghastor**,
with doctorates in bio-engineering, biochemistry, genetics, virology and
psychology — and the framing that he is the world's first supervillain. Earlier
canon supplies the release over the Old Financial District, BEV-131 and what the
initials stand for, the uncontrolled spread and the exposure-duration rule, his
capture and imprisonment, that he is still alive, and that he has never given a
motive.

- **The one field that needed care: `custody: Federal`.** Where he is actually
  held is an open question, and two campaign documents disagree about it. Naming
  an authority rather than a facility fills a required field without settling
  anything, and reads as true under either answer. The article says the location
  is withheld and makes that withholding the point.
- **Invented: the epithet "The Father of the Changed".** Canon gives him none.
  It is meant as what the changed population calls him rather than what the
  press coined, and it grants him a paternity over people who did not ask for it,
  which is the uncomfortable part. Easy to replace.
- **Stretched: `territory: The Old Financial District`.** He is not territorial
  and acted once. The field wants a district or "Itinerant", and the district he
  acted over was the least misleading of the options.
- **Invented: `power_source_published: "None. Five doctorates."`** A formulation,
  not a canon phrase.
- **Invented: `associated: []`,** and the article's line that he is recorded as
  having acted alone with the finding still standing. Canon names no associates,
  which is not the same as canon saying he had none.
- **Invented: "Four of them are the virus. The fifth built nothing."** A reading
  of the list Krys gave. The psychology doctorate is left doing nothing on
  purpose, and the article does not explain it.
- **Invented: that the word supervillain entered ordinary use with him,** and
  that his life before June 11, 2028 survives only as a list of qualifications.
  Canon says nothing about his earlier life either way.
- **Invented: "He survived his own release by standing somewhere else."** Canon
  does not place him on the day. It follows from his being uninfected and
  capable of being captured afterward, but it is an inference.
- **Invented: the shape of the official line** — that the public is told he is
  secured, that he will remain so, and that the rest is a matter of security.
- **Cut before publishing:** a claim that BEV-131 was built from published
  science and could be reconstructed by others. It was invented to fill out the
  Capabilities section, and it would have set the setting's threat model and
  touched the designer-variant material. Left out deliberately.
- **Deliberately not answered: where he is held.** The Related block originally
  linked Tartarus Sands Penitentiary, which would have implied the answer on the
  page regardless of what the prose said. Removed. Also absent: any organisation
  with an interest in reaching him.
