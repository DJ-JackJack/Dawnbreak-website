# Notes on The Old Financial District

What was invented here, and what has not been decided. Kept out of the
article: an article states what is there and lets the reader decide what
else they want to know.

- Invented: the rebuild following the original street plan, the low daytime
  occupancy, and the two-population framing. All are readings of canon's
  "cheapest real estate / residents with no other option / shell companies and
  black-budget contractors", not new facts. Overrule freely.
- Invented: "City of Dawnbreak" as the district operator. Canon does not say
  who administers districts, and this field will want a real answer once one
  exists.
- Unresolved: does the reconstruction programme have a name? An article about
  the rebuild itself would need one.
- Unresolved: DCPD presence here. Canon says nothing, so this article says
  nothing, and the quiet-streets description deliberately stops short of
  claiming the district is safe.
