What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

**This article sits next to the campaign's largest secret and must stay clear of
it.**

Krys supplied the business on 2026-09-11 -- engineering focused on medical devices
and implants, a fabrication department, CIN-branded urgent care clinics and
physical therapy practices -- and the founding on 2026-09-12: Rennick J. Cross Sr.
in 1952, passed to his son Rennick J. Cross Jr. in 1994. Earlier canon supplies the
New Haven tower within sight of the Arkon campus, the lobby, the Grid Row clinic
running around the clock, David Dietrich as owner and chief executive having
inherited from his uncle Rennick, Helena Marsh as COO of eleven years, Dietrich's
public interest in sponsoring a team, and CIN as a smaller operation in the same
business as Arkon.

- **Deliberately excluded: that David Dietrich is Corvid.** Also excluded: the
  sealed section of the tower basement, the sealed sublevel beneath the Grid Row
  clinic, and the existence of a team base. Canon is explicit that the persona's
  safety depends on having no traceable employer. The article names Dietrich as
  chief executive, which is public, and stops at the company.
- **Invented: that the Cross in Cross Idea Network is a surname.** A strong
  inference from the founder's name rather than a stated fact, and the article
  leads with it.
- **Invented: that the fabrication arm grew out of needing implant parts made to
  tolerance.** Canon calls CIN "medical-device-turned-fabrication"; the causal
  story is mine.
- **Invented: "three owners in eighty-eight years,"** the clinics being working
  clinics rather than showrooms, and the company pointing out the
  implant-to-rehabilitation link in its own materials.
- **Deliberately left as intention, not roster: the hero team.** Canon makes the
  PCs CIN's official team. The article says only that Dietrich has publicly said
  he intends to sponsor one, because the roster belongs to the players.
- **Deliberately not answered: what contracts CIN wants.**
