# Notes on Oracle Prime

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Canon supplies the broadcast-automation origin, Prime as the visible body rather
than the whole, the hundreds of All Seeing Eye drones, the cameras-before-combat
lineage, the refit for hero work, the watchful-mother campaign and its stated aim,
and Oracle's standing as the acknowledged exception to Pantheon's BEV origins.

- **The heroes template does not fit an artificial intelligence,** and four
  required fields were filled by reasoning rather than by canon:
  `generation: second` (started in 2038, which keeps canon's "first and second
  generations" true across all six), `powered_since` and `active_since` 2038 (the
  refit), and `civilian_name: None`.
- **Adapted: the epithet.** "The watchful mother" is canon's name for Arkon's
  campaign. The article promotes it to the epithet field and says outright that
  Arkon coined this one, which inverts the field's usual rule.
- **Invented: "the body has been rebuilt before."** Canon says Arkon *would*
  rebuild it. Whether it has ever had to is not established.
- **Invented: the framing that Oracle has no June 11,** and the closing
  observation that the drones are cameras belonging to a media company operating
  over a city that company reports on.
- **Deliberately excluded: that Arkon controls Oracle completely, and that it is
  far simpler than it appears.** The article describes what the city can see.
- **Deliberately not answered: where Oracle's server is.**
