# Notes on Witchmark

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

**This article's most important property is what it leaves out.**

Canon supplies Marielle Moss, the oldest member, her age on H-Day, the Arkon games
studio in the centre of the district, the twelve years of credits, her standing as
an adept magician and the only member equipped for magical threats, the intellect
and tactical aptitude behind the field command, the division between her command
and Spotlight's leadership, and her credit as one of Oracle's designers.

- **Deliberately excluded: her real origin.** Canon establishes that Arkon's
  account of her powers is false, that the company bought silence from surviving
  colleagues, and where her abilities actually come from. None of that is in the
  article and none of it belongs in a public repository.
  `power_source_published` carries Arkon's version *attributed to Arkon*, which
  is what that field was written for.
- **Invented: the epithet "The Grown-Up".**
- **Invented: `generation: first`.** Canon assigns her none. Her published origin
  is H-Day, so the field follows the published origin.
- **Invented: `active_since` and `first_recorded` 2028, `base: Citywide`,** and
  that she has been with Pantheon since its founding.
- **Invented: that she is the only member already employed by Arkon before she
  worked for Arkon.** A reading of canon rather than a stated fact, and a good one
  to check.
- **Invented: that nobody has pressed her in print about the years before Arkon,
  and that her public profile is lower than her seniority would predict.** That
  the early record is thin is canon; the press behaviour around it is mine.
