# Notes on Pantheon

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Canon supplies the roster, the 2038 founding, the Arkon sponsorship, Spotlight's
leadership, the top two floors of Olympus Tower, the twelve rooms mirroring the
twelve Olympians with six standing empty, membership decided on power across both
generations, the corporate incentive structure, and Vanguard as the first team.

- **Invented: `founded_by: Arkon Media`.** Canon says Arkon *sponsors* the team.
  Whether the company founded it or signed something already forming is not
  established, and the field forced a choice.
- **Invented: "nothing in the record suggests the role rotates."**
- **Invented: the Herculean-and-the-Old-Financial-District juxtaposition** as a
  single sentence. Both halves are canon; setting them against each other is a
  reading.
- **Deliberately not answered: whether Arkon maps members to specific Olympians,**
  and **what the six empty rooms mean.** Both are open. The article states the
  twelve, states the six, and says Arkon has announced no plan for them.
- **Deliberately excluded: Witchmark's true origin and Arkon's control of
  Oracle.** The article gives the published position and stops.
