# Notes on H-Day

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Canon supplies almost all of it: the date, the district, Erroghastor, what
BEV-131 stands for, the absence of any motive, the exposure-duration rule, the
disproportionate effect on first responders, the district surviving and being
rebuilt, the spread beyond the city, the virus still being active in 2040 with no
confirmed evolution, his capture, and the anniversary's tone.

- **Derived, not invented: June 11, 2028 was a Sunday.** Off the calendar. Canon
  gives the date and says nothing about the day, and two other articles now rest
  on this. If a weekday suits the fiction better, this is the thread to pull.
- **Invented: `toll: Never established`,** and the reasons given for it. Canon
  gives no figure. That no figure was ever agreed is my claim, not canon's
  silence speaking for itself.
- **Invented: `status: unresolved`** and the `participants` list, which is a
  choice about which four heroes to name out of everyone who was there.
- **Invented: the scope of the response** -- every emergency service plus the
  volunteer companies from surrounding districts. Canon establishes the
  principle; the sweep is mine, built off Grid Row's volunteer houses.
- **Invented: "He is available to be asked, and he has been asked."**
- **Deliberately not stated: where Erroghastor is held.** Canon says captured,
  imprisoned and alive, and two campaign documents disagree about the location.
