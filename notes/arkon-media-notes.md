# Notes on Arkon Media

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Krys supplied the founding on 2026-09-11: 1911 in New York City, Vance Rittenbough
and Darrel Cocchetti, a small newspaper that followed the people who left New York
to found Dawnbreak and covered the migration and the hardship, with Amelia
Sanabria as chief executive now. Earlier canon supplies Olympus Tower and its
floors, the campus, the Trumpet and its modern form, the hero-sponsorship
incentives, Arkon as one of six corporations funding Tartarus, and Oracle's origin
as a broadcast system.

- **Invented: "a small operation for over a decade."** The gap between 1911 and
  the 1920s migration is arithmetic; calling the paper small throughout is not.
- **Invented: that nobody else covered the migration at that length.**
- **Invented: the building "it owns and is not shy about owning,"** and that the
  campus was built to take public events at scale.
- **Invented: the two-businesses framing** and the observation that they have
  grown into each other.
- **Deliberately excluded: Arkon's total control of Oracle, Oracle's real
  simplicity, and Witchmark's fabricated origin.** The article says Arkon built
  Oracle out of its own broadcast automation, which is true, public, and as far as
  it goes.
- **Deliberately not answered: whether any independent outlet exists in
  Dawnbreak.** The article states that Arkon owns the Trumpet and stops.
