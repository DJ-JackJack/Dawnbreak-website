# Notes on The Daniel DeVito School of Performing Arts

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Krys supplied: the death in 2031, Los Angeles, the runaway garbage truck and the
crowd of high school students, the arrhythmia mistaken for latent powers, the
warning the attempt gave the crowd, the honorary superhero designation, the
school being named for him, and the statue — fourteen feet, Frank Reynolds as
the Trashman, a student class project in 2036, titled *A Hero Nonetheless*.

Everything below is mine.

- **Invented: the month.** Canon says 2031 and no more. June was chosen because
  a heat-induced arrhythmia wants summer. Any warm month works.
- **Invented: Tidewater Commons, and the spot inside it.** The Commons is where
  canon puts the stadium, the arena and the civic buildings, and its own article
  puts what matters on the higher northern ground, so the school sits there
  beside the courts and the auditorium. No canon places the school anywhere.
- **Invented: the school itself, in every particular.** Roughly six hundred
  students, audition intake from every district, four programmes with technical
  production the largest, staff split between career teachers and working
  performers, and the informal nine-year arrangement for using the civic
  auditorium next door.
- **Invented: the graduation detail.** That the school takes the first of the
  four June graduation evenings at the stadium. The four evenings are canon from
  the Tidewater Commons article; which school goes first is not.
- **Invented: the council resolution**, its August date and its unanimous vote.
- **Invented: that the designation carries no authority and that he is its only
  holder.** The second is the load-bearing one. If Dawnbreak has named other
  honorary superheroes, that changes what the gesture means.
- **Invented: the Los Angeles paperwork point** — that the investigation and
  vehicle inspection belong to LA, leaving Dawnbreak's record of the event its
  school is named for as news coverage and a resolution.
- **Invented: the students funding the statue themselves**, and the habit of
  touching it on the way in to auditions.
- **Derived, not invented: his age.** Eighty-six in June 2031, from a real birth
  date of November 1944. The real career details are real.

**A previous draft placed the death in Dawnbreak** during graduation week, and
built the crowd of students out of the stadium ceremonies. Krys corrected it:
Los Angeles, national news, and a city that honoured him from three thousand
miles away. The graduation framing is gone; the article no longer explains the
crowd, because it is not Dawnbreak's crowd to explain.
