# Notes on The Skull and Tome Society

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Krys supplied the Society whole on 2026-09-11: its purpose, the difficulty heroes
have created, branch offices in most major cities each opening onto the Eternal
Library, the absence of legal authority, the leverage of the knowledge hoard, the
central law, the preference for peaceful resolution, and the Black Guard with the
stories every prospective caster hears.

- **Written straight at Krys's direction,** despite the Society being secret
  in-world, because a player is connected to it and the group needs the article.
  The secrecy is content rather than a reason to withhold.
- **Invented: `org_type: order`.** None of the eight existing values fit a body
  that governs by custom and leverage, and "agency" would assert a legal mandate
  it explicitly lacks. Added to `schema.js`.
- **Not canon, merely unsupplied: `founded`, `founded_by` and `leadership`,** all
  reading "Unrecorded". These are gaps, not characterisation.
- **Invented: that the Society does not publish its own history.**
- **Invented: the formulations.** "An order that controls what can be learned has
  no particular need for the power to compel," "the stories are the enforcement,"
  the branches existing to put a door somewhere, and distance being an
  administrative detail. All are my phrasing of Krys's mechanics.
- **Invented: the Black Guard being small and rarely deployed.**
- **Invented: the Dawnbreak framing** -- that H-Day is, for an order whose function
  is deniability, the day the work got much harder. It extends canon's line that
  BEV-131 made the strange undeniable and mainstream.
- **Invented: the Witchmark paragraph.** An application of the Society's law to a
  canon figure, not a canon statement about her.
- **Deliberately not answered: whether Dawnbreak has a branch and where, whether
  the Black Guard is what hunts Corvid, and where BEV-131 sits relative to
  "naturally gifted".** All three are on the open list.
