# Notes on Lackluster

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Canon supplies the lobby and the job interview, the twenty-four civilians held
with no powers, the Green Beret and sniper background, the three children, the
abilities arriving a couple of months later, both powers in full including the
organic/nonorganic inversion and Shadowmask's limits, the public coining "the
Pantheon's Ninja", Arkon adopting it and styling the suit after Hollywood shinobi,
and the nylon and synthetic kevlar mesh that phases with him.

- **Canon, not invented: the epithet.** The only one of the six that did not need
  inventing.
- **Invented: `generation: first`.** Canon gives his age and his H-Day but never
  assigns him a generation. He was powered in 2028 and the profile fits, so the
  field says first.
- **Invented: `active_since` and `first_recorded` 2028, `base: Citywide`,** and
  that he joined Pantheon at its founding in 2038.
- **Invented: that the lobby account does not rest on his own telling,** that his
  service record is public in outline and closed in detail, and that his children
  have been kept out of the coverage.
