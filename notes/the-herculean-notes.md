# Notes on The Herculean

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Canon supplies the firefighter, the response to ground zero with no powers, the
exposure as occupational, the six foot nine and the stony patches and the red
hair, his standing as the most visibly mutated member, the mother and disabled
sister, the Arkon arrangement, the tactical role, the comparison with Spotlight's
invulnerability and strength, the absence of flight, speed and projection, and
first generation.

- **Invented: the epithet "The Red Giant".** The schema requires one; canon has
  none for him.
- **Invented: `active_since` and `first_recorded` 2028, and `base: Citywide`.**
  Canon fixes his generation but never says when he started.
- **Invented: that his H-Day account is corroborated by his company and the
  department, and that his civilian name has been published for years.**
- **Softened on purpose: his reason for working with Arkon.** Canon is explicit
  that the arrangement is a paycheque two dependents rely on. The article calls
  it a working arrangement rather than a cause and leaves the motive out. That is
  a privacy call about a living person in the fiction; overrule it if the record
  should be blunter.
