# Notes on Dawnbreak City Police

What was invented in this article, so it stays easy to overrule. Open questions
live in `open-questions.md` with the campaign material, not in this repo.

Canon supplies the cooperation-as-policy framing, the mixed individual feeling,
the sting of being cast as incompetent, the force-wide countermeasure training and
equipment, the fact that the countermeasures barely close the gap, and the
Metahuman Containment Unit with its militarised gear and power-type weapons.
Aldergate's own article puts the headquarters two streets from the courthouse and
records the 2028 losses on public display.

- **Invented: `founded: 1920s` and `founded_by: City of Dawnbreak`.** The
  department being as old as the city is an assumption.
- **Deliberate: `leadership: Office of the Commissioner`.** Canon says DCPD
  leadership is unnamed on purpose and that no commissioner should be invented.
  Naming the office rather than a person fills a required field without inventing
  the man.
- **Invented: no sheriff to divide work with,** drawn from Dawnbreak being an
  independent Virginia city.
- **Invented: "It buys minutes and occasionally a life,"** and the closing point
  that dramatic calls are a small fraction of the work and nearly all of the
  coverage.
- **Deliberately not answered: where the MCU's power-type intelligence comes
  from,** and **whether any officers are metahumans.**
